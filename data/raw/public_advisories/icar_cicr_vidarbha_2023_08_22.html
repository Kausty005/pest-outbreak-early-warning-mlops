ICAR - Central Institute for Cotton Research, Nagpur

Weekly Cotton Pest Surveillance and Management Advisory - Vidarbha Zone

Publication Date: 2023-08-22

Target Districts: Nagpur, Amravati, Yavatmal, Wardha

Target Crop: Cotton (Gossypium hirsutum)

Pink Bollworm (Pectinophora gossypiella) Field Advisory

In cotton growing belts of Nagpur and Yavatmal districts, adult moth activity observed in pheromone monitoring traps. However, green boll destructive sampling shows damage levels remain strictly below the economic threshold level (below 10% damaged green bolls). Farmers are advised to conduct regular field scouting twice a week. Handpick and destroy rosetted flowers containing early instar larvae. If pheromone trap catches cross 8 moths per trap per night for three consecutive nights, apply neem formulation (Azadirachtin 1500 ppm @ 5 ml/liter) as a preventive measure.

Sucking Pests Management (Jassids and Whiteflies)

In some pockets of Amravati district, mild incidence of jassids and whiteflies noticed due to intermittent dry spells. Conserve natural predators such as Chrysoperla and ladybird beetles. Avoid prophylactic chemical spraying of broad-spectrum synthetic pyrethroids which induce secondary pest resurgence.
