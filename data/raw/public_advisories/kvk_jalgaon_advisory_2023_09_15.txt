KRISHI VIGYAN KENDRA (KVK) - JALGAON DISTRICT
Agricultural Extension Advisory Bulletin - Kharif Season 2023
Publication Date: 2023-09-15
Location: Jalgaon District (Khandesh Zone)
Target Crop: Maize (Zea mays)

SUBJECT: FALL ARMYWORM (SPODOPTERA FRUGIPERDA) SCOUTING AND PREVENTIVE ACTION

Farmers across Jalgaon district cultivating maize in the V4 to V8 vegetative growth stage are advised to maintain active vigilance against the Fall Armyworm (Spodoptera frugiperda).
This is a general preventive advisory. Inspect 20 consecutive maize plants across five different locations in the field. Look inside the central leaf whorl for fresh sawdust-like frass and small pinhole perforations.
If whorl damage is observed in less than 5% of plants, hand-picking of egg masses and application of sand or wood ash in the whorl is recommended.
Install pheromone traps at 5 traps per hectare for continuous adult moth monitoring.
Do not spray synthetic pyrethroids indiscriminately.
Issued by: Subject Matter Specialist (Plant Protection), KVK Jalgaon.
