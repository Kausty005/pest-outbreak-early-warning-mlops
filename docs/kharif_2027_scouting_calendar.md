## 1. Calendar Audit & Reconciliation Note (Phase 17 Review)

During the Phase 17 operational audit, a critical temporal discrepancy was identified in the Phase 15 preliminary calendar (`AGRI-CAL-KHARIF-2027-V1`):
1. **The Phase 15 Schedule Truncation:** The preliminary schedule only mapped 16 total calendar weeks (SMW 24 to SMW 39: Jun 11 to Sep 30, 2027), completely omitting the month of October.
2. **Agronomic Impact of Truncation:** In Maharashtra, cotton remains in the field throughout October for late boll opening, second/third pickings, and diapausing Pink Bollworm generations. Ending surveillance on September 30 would miss the peak annual locule damage surge.
3. **Reconciled 20-Week Design:** To span the full June 15 – October 31 period specified in the pilot protocol, the calendar is formally expanded to **20 weeks**:
   - **Phase 1: Baseline Establishment & Trap Setup (Weeks 1–4, SMW 24–27):** Land prep, sowing, soil baseline, pheromone trap installation, early seedling emergence.
   - **Phase 2: Intensive Weekly Field Surveillance (Weeks 5–20, SMW 28–43):** 16 consecutive weekly scouting visits tracking squaring, bloom, destructive boll dissection, meter-row beats, FAW whorl dissections, and BPH culm counts.
   - **Season Closure & Trap Decommissioning (SMW 44):** Final harvest assessment, trap removal, and stalk destruction residue audit.

## 3. Disruption and Weather SOPs

1. **Standard Wednesday Cadence:** Maintains uniform 7-day intervals for time-series modeling ($T \rightarrow T+7$).
2. **$\pm 48\text{ Hour}$ Contingency Window:** Visits delayed by heavy rains ($>50\text{ mm}$) or clay waterlogging must occur between Monday and Friday of the same SMW.
3. **Missed-Visit Recording:** If a plot remains impassable through Friday, the visit is recorded as `NULL` with reason `MISSED_VISIT_WATERLOGGING`. **Zero-imputation is strictly barred.**
4. **Lure Replacement Precision:** Pheromone lures are replaced strictly every **21 calendar days** (Cycles 1 through 7). Damaged or flooded traps must be replaced within 24 hours.
