## 2. Research Background

We are developing **AgriAdapt AI**, a machine learning–based agricultural advisory system for smallholder farmers in Vidarbha and Khandesh, Maharashtra.

The **Pest Outbreak Early Warning Module** aims to:

1. Predict the probability of cotton bollworm pest outbreaks within a **seven-day forecasting window** using environmental and phenological features.
2. Provide crop-specific, district-level early alerts to assist extension workers and KVK field staff in pre-emptive intervention planning.
3. Apply a scientifically defensible two-tier architecture — combining environmental favorability assessments with field surveillance ground-truth — to produce probabilistic outbreak risk scores.

> **This is an academic research project.** No commercial application or revenue generation is involved.

---

## 3. Why ICAR-CICR Data is Critical

ICAR-CICR is the foremost authority on cotton pest management in the central cotton growing zone. Specifically:

- ICAR-CICR maintains **weekly pheromone trap catch records (moths/trap/night)** for Pink Bollworm (*Pectinophora gossypiella*) and American Bollworm (*Helicoverpa armigera*) across Vidarbha Vertisol cotton districts.
- ICAR-CICR conducts **destructive green boll dissection surveys** that provide verified bollworm infestation percentages.
- Multi-year records from ICAR-CICR represent the **gold-standard ground-truth** for training and validating any machine learning–based cotton pest forecasting model in this region.

Without access to authentic ICAR-CICR field records, our model cannot be trained on verified entomological data, and any predictions would remain scientifically unvalidated.
