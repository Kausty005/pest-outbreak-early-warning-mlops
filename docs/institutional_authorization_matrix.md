## 1. Executive Summary & Regulatory Context

Acquiring authentic agricultural pest surveillance data in India requires formal bilateral institutional authorization. Unsanctioned data scraping, unofficial file sharing, or unauthorized researcher data dumps violate institutional data sovereignty, academic ethics, and public sector information security regulations.

During Phase 12, six tailored institutional engagement packages were formulated. To ensure complete organizational compliance, this **Institutional Authorization Matrix** defines the mandatory administrative prerequisites, required institutional endorsements, data-use boundaries, and submission workflows for each candidate institution.

> [!WARNING]
> **Zero Dispatched Requests:** No institutional request, email, or formal petition has been submitted. All six dossiers are locked in draft status pending institutional principal investigator endorsement and academic committee review.

## 3. Mandatory Multi-Step Submission Workflow & Follow-Up Procedures

Prior to dispatching any institutional request, the following multi-stage administrative workflow must be executed:

```
[ Step 1: Internal Proposal Review ]
  │  AgriAdapt AI Project Team finalizes proposal and technical architecture.
  ▼
[ Step 2: Academic Supervisor & Department Sign-Off ]
  │  Faculty Supervisor, Head of Department, and Dean review and sign formal letters.
  ▼
[ Step 3: University Institutional Review Board / Ethics Clearance ]
  │  Ethics committee approves farmer consent, data privacy, and field indemnity protocols.
  ▼
[ Step 4: Physical Document Assembly & Institutional Seal ]
  │  Formal letters printed on university letterhead, signed, and embossed with official seal.
  ▼
[ Step 5: Official Dispatch & Dossier Tracking ]
  │  Registered postal dispatch / official in-person submission to target Director / Commissioner.
  │  Speed Post tracking numbers and institutional diary numbers logged in Provenance Registry.
  ▼
[ Step 6: 30-Day Formal Review & Bilateral Follow-Up ]
  │  Designated 30-day review period by institutional PME / Technical Committee.
  │  Formal academic follow-up conducted exclusively through University Registrar.
  ▼
[ Step 7: Bilateral Data Sharing Agreement (DSA) Execution ]
  │  Formal DSA signed by institutional Registrars prior to any data transmission.
```
