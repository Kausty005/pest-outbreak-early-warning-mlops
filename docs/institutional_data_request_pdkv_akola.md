Dr. PDKV Akola is the pre-eminent agricultural university serving the 11 districts of the Vidarbha region. We seek formal academic collaboration with the Department of Agricultural Entomology:
1. **Entomological Threshold Validation:** Expert review and verification of Economic Threshold Levels (ETL) used in our forward-window labeling logic for Vidarbha vertisols.

3. **Prospective Kharif 2027 Field Pilot:** Joint coordination of university sentinel plots and validation of our mobile/web scouting data collection protocol.
4. **Joint Scientific Publication:** Co-authorship for participating PDKV entomologists and research scholars in high-impact agricultural informatics and plant protection journals.
