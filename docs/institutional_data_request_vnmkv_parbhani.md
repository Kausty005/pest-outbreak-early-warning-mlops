### 2. Research Context & Purpose

VNMKV Parbhani is recognized for pioneering research on Fall Armyworm (*Spodoptera frugiperda*) management in maize and integrated pest management in cotton across the Marathwada and Khandesh agro-climatic zones.

Under the **AgriAdapt AI** open-source initiative, we are developing an explainable 7-day pest outbreak early-warning system. We seek academic research collaboration to:
1. **Model Fall Armyworm in Maize:** Calibrate environmental favorability models with authentic field scouting whorl damage observations and pheromone trap catches.
