### 2. Research Background & Public Interest Purpose

Yavatmal is historically one of Maharashtra's most intensive cotton-producing districts, frequently subject to severe Pink Bollworm (*Pectinophora gossypiella*) infestation cycles. Under the **AgriAdapt AI** research initiative, we are developing an explainable, open-source early-warning system capable of forecasting pest favorability and outbreak risk with a 7-day lead time.

By coupling antecedent micro-meteorological variables (ERA5-Land topsoil moisture and open weather reanalysis) with verified field scouting, this system aims to assist local agricultural extension officers and KVK advisors in recommending timely, pre-emptive interventions before economic injury occurs.

### 3. Specific Data & Field Collaboration Requested

We request collaboration on:
1. **Target Crops:** Cotton (*Gossypium hirsutum*, Bt BG-II).
2. **Target Pests:**
   - Cotton Pink Bollworm (*Pectinophora gossypiella*): Green boll dissection damage %, rosette flower incidence %, pheromone trap catches.
   - American Bollworm (*Helicoverpa armigera*): Square damage %, flared bracteoles, trap counts.
3. **Temporal Horizon:**
   - Historical records: Kharif 2021, 2022, 2023, and 2024 from Yavatmal talukas (e.g., Darwha, Pusad, Ghatanji, Kalamb).
   - Prospective pilot: Establishing 5 sentinel monitoring fields in Yavatmal for the **Kharif 2027 Field Pilot** (July–October 2027).
4. **Data Format:** CSV or structured field logs capturing observation date, village, phenological stage, plants sampled, plants affected, incidence %, and trap counts.
